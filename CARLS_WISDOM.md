## About this collection

These are edited study notes, with Carl's advice paraphrased and short direct quotations identified. The September email is an instructor reference; later in-class instructions and shop rules take priority. External quotations are followed by a source link and an explanation of their relevance to our table. Link checks are dated **8 October 2026**; a blocked page is labeled as unverified, rather than treated as evidence.

Robert's [CCAE Woodworking Shaker Table site](https://sites.google.com/view/ccae-woodworking-shaker-table/home) was a useful companion comparison. Our collection is organized directly from Carl's PDF and the source plans. Topic pages stay one level below this index, and each has its own contents list and a route back here.

See [Sources and Link Index](CARLS_WISDOM_SOURCES.md) for the PDF page map, original links, replacements, and access notes. The original email's personal contact details and recipient list remain in the local archive. Full source PDFs and full-page renders are also kept locally; selected study illustrations retain their original attribution in [Attribution](ATTRIBUTION.md).
