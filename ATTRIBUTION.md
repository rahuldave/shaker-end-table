# Attribution

## Carl's Wisdom collection

The Carl's Wisdom pages are edited study notes based on **Carl Spagnoli's class email dated 23 September 2026** and his annotations in the comparative Woodsmith plan. Carl is credited for his teaching advice and reading selections; repetitions are consolidated and project applications are identified as commentary. The original email's personal recipient and contact details remain local.

The selected files `images/carl-wisdom/woodsmith-jig-layout.jpg` and `woodsmith-jig-hold-down.jpg` are figures 6 and 8 from the **Shaker Hall Table** downloadable plan, local PDF p. 12 / printed p. 10. `woodsmith-jointer-cut-reference.jpg` is figure 5 from local PDF p. 14 / printed p. 12 (also marked magazine p. 32). These figures are © 2007 August Home Publishing Co.; they are included for educational commentary and retain the source's rights. The old source URL printed in the PDF is [woodsmithshop.com/download/104/104-shakerhalltable.pdf](https://www.woodsmithshop.com/download/104/104-shakerhalltable.pdf), which returned 404 on 8 October 2026. The publisher's [issue 61 index](https://www.woodsmith.com/magazine/issue/61/) identifies the hall-table and related technique articles. Full source PDFs and full-page renders are not redistributed.

Carl's restriction on independent beginner use of jointer tapering appears prominently beside that selected figure on the taper page. The diagrams' four-face hall-table dimensions and jig setup are not adopted for our two-face end-table taper.

`images/carl-wisdom/two-face-taper-layout.svg` is a project-created educational diagram using Schwarz's retained leg/taper targets. It is not an extracted magazine drawing and is not to scale. Original source: [Schwarz's drawing and taper discussion, PDF pp. 3–5](https://www.popularwoodworking.com/app/uploads/2010/10/HiRes-SEPT2004-Seg2.pdf#page=4).

Short external quotations are attributed beside their text and linked to the original authors, publishers, manufacturers, or lumber suppliers. They remain the words of their respective sources. [Sources and Link Index](CARLS_WISDOM_SOURCES.md) gives access notes and the PDF page map. Robert's [CCAE Woodworking Shaker Table site](https://sites.google.com/view/ccae-woodworking-shaker-table/home) was consulted as a companion class reference.

## Original project sources

This educational project reference is based on:

> Christopher Schwarz, "Simple Shaker End Table," *Woodworking Magazine*, Autumn 2004, pages 16-21.

The original publication is available from [Popular Woodworking](https://www.popularwoodworking.com/app/uploads/2010/10/HiRes-SEPT2004-Seg2.pdf).

The selected photographs and dimensioned drawing under `images/` were extracted from that publication for commentary, study, and class-project reference. The article, photographs, and illustrations remain the property of their respective authors, photographers, publisher, and copyright holders. They are not offered under an open-source license by this repository.

The project notes summarize and interpret the source; they are not a transcription of the article.

The cutting-diagram page also draws on David Thiel's "Gluing up Flat Panels" (printed pages 22-23) and "Simple & Fast Rabbeted Drawers" (printed pages 24-27) in the same publisher PDF. The cutting illustrations (`top-cutting-example.svg`, `remaining-cherry-cutting.svg`, `leg-cutting-example.svg`, `leg-square-stock-example.svg`, and `drawer-cutting-example.svg`) are project-created planning illustrations using hypothetical board dimensions and proposed rough allowances; they are not extracted magazine illustrations. Their table-part baselines follow Schwarz's design. Those September illustrations are archived examples; the drawer was omitted from the current build on 2026-10-08. Other explanatory SVG diagrams are project-created educational aids, not extracted source photographs or drawings.

The October 2026 drawings (`purchased-poplar-board-1.svg`, `purchased-poplar-board-2.svg`, `purchased-leg-blanks-2026-10-08.svg`, `drawer-free-top-and-base.svg` and `whole-poplar-table-cutaway.svg`) are project-created allocations based on reported purchased poplar sizes, with unmeasured dimensions explicitly identified. The fourth front apron is a project adaptation of the source rear-apron joint. `mortise-tenon-corner.png` is a selected crop of the original dimensioned drawing on PDF page 4; copyright remains with the source rights holders.
