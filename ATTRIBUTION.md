# Attribution

This educational project reference is based on:

> Christopher Schwarz, "Simple Shaker End Table," *Woodworking Magazine*, Autumn 2004, pages 16-21.

The original publication is available from [Popular Woodworking](https://www.popularwoodworking.com/app/uploads/2010/10/HiRes-SEPT2004-Seg2.pdf).

The selected photographs and dimensioned drawing under `images/` were extracted from that publication for commentary, study, and class-project reference. The article, photographs, and illustrations remain the property of their respective authors, photographers, publisher, and copyright holders. They are not offered under an open-source license by this repository.

The project notes summarize and interpret the source; they are not a transcription of the article.

The cutting-diagram page also draws on David Thiel's "Gluing up Flat Panels" (printed pages 22-23) and "Simple & Fast Rabbeted Drawers" (printed pages 24-27) in the same publisher PDF. The cutting illustrations (`top-cutting-example.svg`, `remaining-cherry-cutting.svg`, `leg-cutting-example.svg`, `leg-square-stock-example.svg`, and `drawer-cutting-example.svg`) are project-created planning illustrations using hypothetical board dimensions and proposed rough allowances; they are not extracted magazine illustrations. Their table-part baselines follow Schwarz's design. Our selected dovetailed drawer is to be fitted to the assembled opening rather than cut from the article's rabbeted-drawer dimensions. Other explanatory SVG diagrams are project-created educational aids, not extracted source photographs or drawings.
