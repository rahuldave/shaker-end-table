# Carl's Wisdom: Sources and Link Index

The collection begins with **Carl Spagnoli's class email of 23 September 2026**, supplied as an 18-page PDF, and his annotated 14-page **Woodsmith Shaker Hall Table** attachment. The local PDFs are preserved unchanged. Page numbers below refer to the PDF viewer, with printed numbers distinguished where useful.

Original links were followed on **8 October 2026**, including all eight pages of Robert's companion site. Useful primary sources were read more deeply for the topic pages' short quotations. A successful page load is a link check, not confirmation of current store inventory, a machine procedure, or every claim on that site. Access failures are identified below; older URLs that redirect successfully are represented by the usable destination where known.

## Wood, suppliers, and terminology

**Material science and species.** [The Wood Database](https://www.wood-database.com/) and its [color-change article](https://www.wood-database.com/wood-articles/preventing-color-changes-in-exotic-woods/); [Forest Products Laboratory](https://research.fs.usda.gov/fpl), [Wood Handbook index](https://research.fs.usda.gov/fpl/wood-handbook), and [full publication](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/62200). These loaded. For our poplar, we additionally followed the Database's [yellow-poplar entry](https://www.wood-database.com/yellow-poplar/) and the Handbook's [moisture chapter](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/62243).

**Dealers.** [Anderson & McQuaid](https://www.andersonmcquaid.com/price-list/), [Woodcraft](https://www.woodcraft.com/categories/dimensional-lumber), [Longleaf](https://www.longleaflumber.com/), [Highland Hardwoods](https://www.highlandhardwoods.com/), [Sterritt](https://www.sterrittlumber.com/our-products), [Arlington Coal & Lumber](https://www.arlcoal.com/), [Moynihan](https://moynihanlumber.com/), [Rockler domestic lumber](https://www.rockler.com/wood/domestic-lumber), and [Ocooch](https://ocoochhardwoods.com/) loaded. Ocooch's [wood-care guide](https://ocoochhardwoods.com/care-of-wood/) and [FAQ](https://ocoochhardwoods.com/faq/) were followed too. Carl's food detour, [Iggy's](https://www.iggysbread.com/), loaded.

**Boulter access note.** Carl's [old hardwood-lumber page](https://www.boulterplywood.com/HardwoodLumber_4.htm) rejected automated access, and its HTTP version also failed. The [homepage](https://www.boulterplywood.com/) was checked but did not supply a readable catalogue in this environment. Do not infer stock or prices from these checks.

**Terminology.** Carl's links to [Maker Gray](https://www.makergray.com/concrete/woodworking-terms), [This Old House](https://www.thisoldhouse.com/woodworking/the-language-of-lumber), [Obsessed Woodworking](https://www.obsessedwoodworking.com/woodworking-glossary/), [Hood Distribution](https://hooddistribution.com/resources/common-lumber-terms/), and [Cline Lumber](https://clinelumber.com/about/basic-lumber-terminology/) loaded. Our technical quotation and dealer terminology notes use the primary dealer sources Hood and Cline. Use the [project glossary](LUMBER_AND_WOODWORKING_TERMINOLOGY.md) to relate the terms to our parts.

## Attribution and editorial boundaries

Carl's advice is credited to his dated class email and attachment annotations. The public notes summarize the substantive woodworking advice and group his wider reading; repetitions are consolidated. Personal recipient addresses, phone numbers, and contact details are omitted from the public pages.

Christopher Schwarz and *Woodworking Magazine* remain the source of the intended table. Woodsmith and August Home Publishing remain the source of the comparative plan and selected jig/jointer figures. Full-page copies are not redistributed. See [Attribution](ATTRIBUTION.md) for the selected-image credits and original source URLs.

Two editorial corrections are explicit: the email's four-times-13 arithmetic is explained on [Shaker Table](CARLS_WISDOM_SHAKER_TABLE.md#the-cut-list-homework), and its apparent Darwin-title slip is identified as an inference on [Beyond the Bench](CARLS_WISDOM_BEYOND.md#poetry-and-wider-reading). No dimensions, joint system, or finish have been silently changed by this collection.
